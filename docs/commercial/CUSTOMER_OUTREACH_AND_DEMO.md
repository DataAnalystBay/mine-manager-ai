\# Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 — Customer Outreach and Demo Package



Version: 1.0.0

Commercial Branch: commercial/v1.0-pilot

Purpose: Standard outreach and first customer demo structure



\## 1. Target Customer Roles



Primary decision-makers:



\- General Manager

\- Mine Manager

\- Technical Services Manager

\- Planning Manager

\- Operations Manager

\- Superintendent



Secondary stakeholders:



\- Technology / Data Manager

\- Business Improvement Manager

\- Reporting / Analytics Lead

\- Finance or Commercial Manager



\## 2. Ideal First Customer



The strongest first pilot customer has:



\- A real operational reporting pain point

\- Significant manual Excel or PowerPoint reporting

\- Management demand for faster operational visibility

\- Accessible production, fleet, plant and safety data

\- A senior sponsor willing to participate in the pilot

\- Ability to provide structured feedback

\- Potential to become a reference customer

\- Potential for paid conversion after pilot

\## 4. First Meeting Goal



The goal of the first meeting is not to sell every feature.



The goal is to determine whether the customer has a real problem that Version 1.0 can solve.



Questions to understand:



1\. How is the daily operational report prepared today?

2\. How many people contribute to the report?

3\. How much time is spent preparing it?

4\. Which KPIs are most important to management?

5\. Where do inconsistent numbers occur?

6\. Which systems or spreadsheets currently hold the data?

7\. How quickly can management identify underperforming areas?

8\. What reports are prepared daily, weekly and monthly?

9\. What would make management trust an automated reporting platform?

10\. Who would sponsor a pilot?



\## 5. Recommended Demo Duration



Total duration:



15 to 20 minutes



Suggested structure:



\### Minute 0–2 — Problem



Explain the reporting challenge:



Mining managers often receive information from multiple spreadsheets, systems and teams.



The problem is not lack of data.



The problem is turning operational data into a consistent management view quickly enough to support decisions.



\### Minute 2–5 — Executive Dashboard



Show:



\- Mine Health Score

\- Ore performance

\- Waste performance

\- Fleet performance

\- Plant performance

\- Safety performance

\- Risks

\- Priority actions



Primary message:



One management view of operational performance.



\### Minute 5–8 — Executive KPI Analysis



Show:



\- KPI current status

\- Historical trend

\- KPI drivers

\- Root causes

\- Executive recommendations



Primary message:



The platform helps explain why performance changed.



\### Minute 8–11 — AI Executive Insights



Show:



\- Executive summary

\- Operational issues

\- Risk commentary

\- Recommended actions



Primary message:



The system converts operational data into management-level commentary.



\### Minute 11–14 — Predictive Intelligence



Show:



\- Next-shift forecast

\- Three-shift forecast

\- Forecast direction

\- Confidence

\- Executive forecast message



Primary message:



Mine Manager AI does not only describe the past; it provides forward-looking operational context.



\### Minute 14–17 — Executive Reporting



Show:



\- Daily Executive PDF

\- Weekly Operations PDF

\- Monthly KPI Pack

\- Excel Export

\- PowerPoint Board Pack



Primary message:



Management reporting can be generated directly from the same operational intelligence layer.



\### Minute 17–20 — Pilot Discussion



Explain:



\- 4 to 8 week pilot

\- Customer provides sample operational data

\- KPI definitions are aligned

\- Platform is configured to the mine

\- Results are measured

\- Commercial conversion is discussed after the pilot



\## 6. Demo Rules



During the first demo:



\- Use synthetic data unless customer data has been formally approved

\- Do not expose another customer's information

\- Do not promise Version 2.0 functionality

\- Do not promise direct integrations that are outside Version 1.0

\- Do not claim predictive outputs are guaranteed forecasts

\- Clearly explain that AI outputs support management decisions rather than replace accountable operational leadership



\## 7. Core Product Message



Mine Manager AI is not positioned as:



"ChatGPT for mining."



Preferred positioning:



"Executive operations intelligence and decision-support for mining management."



Core value proposition:



Mine Manager AI turns operational mining data into a consistent executive view of performance, risks, forecasts and priority actions while reducing manual management-reporting effort.



\## 8. Pilot Call to Action



Recommended close:



"If the workflow looks useful for your management team, the next step would be a controlled 4 to 8 week pilot. We would start with a small sample of your production, fleet, plant and safety data, align the KPI definitions, and measure whether the platform improves reporting efficiency and management visibility."
