\# Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 — Commercial Pricing Framework



Version: 1.0.0

Commercial Branch: commercial/v1.0-pilot

Purpose: Initial pricing framework for pilot and post-pilot commercial discussions



\## 1. Pricing Principles



Mine Manager AI should be priced based on customer value, deployment scope, support requirements, and number of mines rather than only software-development cost.



The commercial model should remain simple during early pilots.



\## 2. Pilot Pricing



\### Option A — Paid Pilot



Recommended range:



USD 5,000 to 15,000



Typical duration:



4 to 8 weeks



Includes:



\- Customer onboarding

\- Environment setup

\- Company and mine configuration

\- Data mapping

\- KPI setup

\- User setup

\- Dashboard configuration

\- Predictive Intelligence

\- Executive reports

\- Pilot support

\- Final pilot review



\### Option B — Strategic Reference Pilot



Recommended range:



USD 0 to 5,000



Use only when the customer has strong strategic value.



Examples:



\- Major mining company

\- Strong reference potential

\- Access to senior decision-makers

\- High probability of paid conversion

\- Strong potential for multiple-mine expansion



A free or discounted pilot should require clear customer commitments.



\## 3. Post-Pilot Annual Pricing



\### Starter Deployment



Recommended range:



USD 15,000 to 30,000 per mine per year



Suitable for:



\- Small mine

\- Limited user group

\- Standard Version 1.0 functionality

\- File-upload workflow

\- Standard support



\### Professional Deployment



Recommended range:



USD 30,000 to 60,000 per mine per year



Suitable for:



\- Medium to large mine

\- Management and superintendent users

\- Full Version 1.0 feature set

\- Configuration support

\- Regular reporting support

\- Priority technical support



\### Enterprise Deployment



Recommended starting range:



USD 60,000 to 120,000+ per mine per year



Suitable for:



\- Large mining operations

\- Multiple management teams

\- Higher support requirements

\- Customer-specific configuration

\- Advanced security/deployment requirements

\- Future integration requirements



\## 4. Multi-Mine Pricing



For mining groups with multiple operations, pricing may use a group license.



Example structure:



First mine:

100% of standard annual price



Second mine:

80% of standard annual price



Third and additional mines:

60% to 75% of standard annual price



Final pricing should depend on implementation effort and support requirements.



\## 5. Setup and Implementation Fee



Separate setup fees may be charged for:



\- Initial deployment

\- Data mapping

\- Historical data preparation

\- KPI configuration

\- User onboarding

\- Branding

\- Custom report configuration



Suggested range:



USD 3,000 to 15,000



\## 6. Support Options



\### Standard Support



Included in annual subscription.



May include:



\- Email support

\- Bug fixes

\- Security updates

\- Version 1.0.x maintenance releases



\### Premium Support



Additional annual fee.



May include:



\- Priority support

\- Scheduled operational review

\- Faster response times

\- Configuration assistance

\- Reporting support



Suggested premium support fee:



10% to 20% of annual software fee



\## 7. Custom Development



Customer-specific work outside standard Version 1.0 scope should be priced separately.



Examples:



\- SAP integration

\- PI System integration

\- Dispatch integration

\- Custom database connectors

\- Customer-specific workflows

\- New report types

\- Major customer-specific features



Pricing should be based on scope, effort, risk, and long-term support obligation.



\## 8. Discount Policy



Discounts may be used for:



\- First reference customers

\- Multi-mine commitments

\- Annual prepayment

\- Strategic mining partners



Avoid permanent discounts that reduce future pricing power.



\## 9. Commercial Conversion Target



A successful pilot should aim to convert into at least one of:



\- Annual subscription

\- Annual enterprise license

\- Multi-mine agreement

\- Paid implementation project



\## 10. Early Revenue Targets



Initial objective:



\- 2 pilot customers

\- At least 1 paid conversion

\- Establish reference customers

\- Validate willingness to pay

\- Establish repeatable pricing
