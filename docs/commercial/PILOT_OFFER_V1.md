\# Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 — Pilot Offer



Version: 1.0.3

Commercial Branch: commercial/v1.0-pilot

Purpose: First customer pilot proposal



\## 1. Pilot Objective



The purpose of the pilot is to demonstrate that Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 can reduce manual operational reporting effort, improve executive visibility, and provide useful decision-support using customer mine data.



\## 2. Pilot Duration



Recommended pilot duration:



4 to 8 weeks



Suggested structure:



Week 1:

\- Customer onboarding

\- Data review

\- KPI definition alignment

\- Environment configuration



Week 2:

\- Data mapping

\- Initial dashboard setup

\- User configuration

\- Report validation



Weeks 3 to 6:

\- Operational use

\- KPI accuracy review

\- Executive reporting use

\- Predictive Intelligence review

\- Customer feedback collection



Final Week:

\- Pilot acceptance review

\- Business-value assessment

\- Commercial conversion discussion



\## 3. Mine Manager AI Deliverables



The pilot includes:



\- Customer company and mine configuration

\- User and role setup

\- Production analytics

\- Fleet analytics

\- Plant analytics

\- Safety analytics

\- Mine Health Score

\- Executive Dashboard

\- Executive KPI Analysis

\- AI Executive Insights

\- Predictive Intelligence

\- Executive Actions

\- Daily Executive PDF Report

\- Weekly Operations PDF Report

\- Monthly KPI Pack

\- Executive Excel Export

\- Executive PowerPoint Board Pack

\- Report History

\- Audit Trail

\- System Health

\- Support during the pilot



\## 4. Customer Responsibilities



The customer provides:



\- Pilot sponsor

\- Primary business contact

\- Technical/data contact

\- Production sample data

\- Fleet sample data

\- Plant sample data

\- Safety sample data

\- KPI definitions

\- KPI targets and thresholds

\- Shift configuration

\- Reporting requirements

\- User list

\- Timely feedback during the pilot



\## 5. Data Requirements



Preferred initial data period:



30 to 90 days



Minimum expected data:



\- Production

\- Fleet

\- Plant

\- Safety



Optional data:



\- Maintenance

\- Workforce



Customer data remains customer-owned.



Real customer operational data must not be committed to the Mine Manager AI source-code repository.



\## 6. Pilot Success Criteria



The pilot will be considered successful when:



\- Customer data loads reliably

\- Core KPI calculations are accepted by the customer

\- Executive Dashboard is useful to management

\- Executive reports reduce manual preparation effort

\- AI Executive Insights are understandable and relevant

\- Predictive Intelligence provides useful forward-looking context

\- No unresolved Critical release issues remain

\- Customer management confirms measurable business value



\## 7. Pilot Performance Measures



Recommended measures:



\- Reporting preparation time before pilot

\- Reporting preparation time after pilot

\- Manual spreadsheet effort reduction

\- Number of management reports automated

\- KPI accuracy acceptance rate

\- Number of active pilot users

\- Executive report usage

\- Customer satisfaction rating

\- Management decision-support usefulness rating



\## 8. Pilot Support



Mine Manager AI provides:



\- Initial setup support

\- Data mapping support

\- Configuration support

\- Bug fixes for pilot-blocking Version 1.0 issues

\- Reporting accuracy fixes

\- Technical troubleshooting

\- Scheduled pilot review meetings



\## 9. Scope Control



Version 1.0 pilot work may include:



\- Critical bug fixes

\- Security fixes

\- Data accuracy fixes

\- Customer-blocking usability fixes

\- Deployment fixes

\- Report accuracy fixes

\- Configuration changes



The pilot does not include:



\- Executive Decision Center

\- AI Mine Operations Copilot

\- Native mobile application

\- Direct SAP integration

\- Direct PI System integration

\- Direct dispatch-system integration

\- Large customer-specific integrations

\- Version 2.0 features
