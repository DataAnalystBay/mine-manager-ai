\# Mine Manager AI V1.0.3 Daily Operating Workflow



\## Objective



Provide a consistent daily operating routine for mine leadership using Mine Manager AI.



\## Before Upload



Confirm:



\- Correct mine selected

\- Reporting date correct

\- Production data reviewed

\- Fleet data reviewed

\- Plant data reviewed

\- Safety data reviewed

\- Files use approved Version 1.0.3 templates



\## Upload Sequence



Upload in this order:



1\. Production

2\. Fleet

3\. Plant

4\. Safety



Confirm each upload succeeds.



Target:



Data Completeness = 100%



\## Management Review Sequence



After upload:



1\. Generate AI Daily Briefing

2\. Open Executive Dashboard

3\. Review Mine Health

4\. Review Production performance

5\. Review Fleet performance

6\. Review Plant performance

7\. Review Safety performance

8\. Review AI Executive Insights

9\. Review Predictive Intelligence

10\. Review Priority Actions



\## Action Management



For material exceptions:



\- Create or confirm an Executive Action

\- Assign an accountable owner

\- Assign timing or due date

\- Confirm expected benefit

\- Track status

\- Escalate overdue or blocked actions



\## Daily Report



Generate the Daily Executive Report after the operational data and actions have been reviewed.



Use the report for:



\- Daily operations meeting

\- General Manager briefing

\- Mine Manager review

\- Superintendent coordination

\- Shift or leadership handover



\## Daily Completion Criteria



The daily Mine Manager AI cycle is complete when:



\- \[ ] Four operational datasets uploaded

\- \[ ] Data Completeness is 100%

\- \[ ] Dashboard reviewed

\- \[ ] AI Daily Briefing reviewed

\- \[ ] Material risks reviewed

\- \[ ] Actions assigned

\- \[ ] Daily Executive Report generated
