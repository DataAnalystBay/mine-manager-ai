\# Mine Manager AI Version 1.0.3 Quick Start Guide



\## Purpose



Mine Manager AI helps mining leaders convert operational data into executive KPIs, management insights, risks, actions, predictive intelligence, and executive reports.



Version 1.0.3 uses daily Production, Fleet, Plant, and Safety data.



\## 1. Sign In



Open the customer Mine Manager AI URL in a supported web browser.



Enter your assigned email address and password.



Your available functions depend on your assigned role.



Supported roles:



\- Administrator

\- General Manager

\- Mine Manager

\- Superintendent

\- Viewer



Viewer accounts are read-only and cannot upload operational data.



\## 2. Select or Confirm Mine Configuration



Before uploading data, confirm:



\- Company

\- Mine

\- Timezone

\- Language

\- KPI targets



Contact the system Administrator if any configuration is incorrect.



\## 3. Prepare Daily Operational Files



Use the approved Version 1.0.3 templates.



\### Production



Required columns:



\- report\_date

\- ore\_plan

\- ore\_actual

\- waste\_plan

\- waste\_actual



\### Fleet



Required columns:



\- report\_date

\- availability

\- utilization



\### Plant



Required columns:



\- report\_date

\- throughput\_plan

\- throughput\_actual

\- recovery



\### Safety



Required columns:



\- report\_date

\- incidents

\- near\_misses

\- critical\_risks

\- safety\_score



Do not rename required columns.



Use one row per reporting date.



\## 4. Upload Operational Data



Open:



Upload Reports



Upload the four operational files:



1\. Production

2\. Fleet

3\. Plant

4\. Safety



Confirm each file displays a successful upload result.



Verify Data Completeness reaches 100%.



\## 5. Generate AI Daily Briefing



After all required operational data is uploaded:



Click:



Generate AI Daily Briefing



Review the generated summary before using it in management meetings.



\## 6. Review Executive Dashboard



Open:



Dashboard



Review:



\- Mine Health

\- Ore Production

\- Waste Movement

\- Fleet Performance

\- Plant Performance

\- Safety Performance

\- Priority Actions

\- AI Executive Insights

\- Predictive Intelligence



Investigate any KPI that is below target or shows a material risk.



\## 7. Review Executive Actions



Open:



Executive Actions



Review:



\- Priority

\- Action

\- Owner

\- Timing

\- Status



Assign clear ownership and due dates to material actions.



Update action status as work progresses.



\## 8. Generate Executive Reports



Open:



Executive Reports



Available outputs include:



\- Daily Executive Report PDF

\- Weekly Operations Report PDF

\- Monthly KPI Pack PDF

\- Executive Excel Export

\- Executive PowerPoint Board Pack



Use the reporting output appropriate for the management cycle.



\## 9. Daily Management Workflow



Recommended daily sequence:



1\. Prepare operational data

2\. Upload Production

3\. Upload Fleet

4\. Upload Plant

5\. Upload Safety

6\. Confirm Data Completeness

7\. Generate AI Daily Briefing

8\. Review Dashboard

9\. Review AI Executive Insights

10\. Review Predictive Intelligence

11\. Update Executive Actions

12\. Generate Daily Executive Report

13\. Conduct daily management review



\## 10. Weekly Management Workflow



Recommended weekly sequence:



1\. Confirm all daily data has been uploaded

2\. Review weekly KPI trends

3\. Review Fleet and Plant constraints

4\. Review weekly Safety exposure

5\. Review Executive Actions

6\. Generate Weekly Operations Report

7\. Confirm accountable owners and recovery actions



\## 11. Monthly Management Workflow



Recommended monthly sequence:



1\. Confirm complete monthly dataset

2\. Review monthly Mine Health

3\. Review production achievement

4\. Review Fleet performance

5\. Review Plant performance

6\. Review Safety exposure

7\. Generate Monthly KPI Pack

8\. Generate Executive Excel Export if required

9\. Generate Board Pack for executive review

10\. Confirm next-month management actions



\## 12. Important Usage Rules



\- Verify source data before upload.

\- Do not change required template column names.

\- Do not upload duplicate reporting dates within the same file.

\- Review AI-generated insights before acting.

\- Mine Manager AI supports management judgment; it does not replace operational accountability.

\- Protect user credentials.

\- Do not share Administrator credentials.

\- Report data-quality or calculation issues immediately.



\## 13. Support



If the system does not behave as expected, record:



\- User name

\- Mine

\- Date and time

\- Screen or module

\- File name

\- Error message

\- Screenshot

\- Steps taken before the issue occurred



Provide this information to the assigned Mine Manager AI support contact.
