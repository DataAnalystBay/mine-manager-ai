\# Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 — Pilot Success Criteria



\## Pilot Goal



Demonstrate that Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 provides measurable operational reporting and decision-support value using customer data.



\## Technical Acceptance



\- \[ ] Customer environment deployed successfully

\- \[ ] Authentication works

\- \[ ] Role-based access works

\- \[ ] Customer data loads successfully

\- \[ ] Dashboard operates without critical errors

\- \[ ] Database remains stable

\- \[ ] System Health reports normal operation



\## Data Acceptance



\- \[ ] KPI definitions agreed with customer

\- \[ ] Production KPIs validated

\- \[ ] Fleet KPIs validated

\- \[ ] Plant KPIs validated

\- \[ ] Safety KPIs validated

\- \[ ] Mine Health calculations reviewed

\- \[ ] Material data-quality issues documented



\## Decision-Support Acceptance



\- \[ ] AI Executive Insights are understandable

\- \[ ] Executive Actions are relevant

\- \[ ] Predictive Intelligence is understandable

\- \[ ] Forecast confidence is visible

\- \[ ] Management users can identify operational priorities



\## Reporting Acceptance



\- \[ ] Daily Executive PDF is useful

\- \[ ] Weekly Operations Report is useful

\- \[ ] Monthly KPI Pack is useful

\- \[ ] Excel export is usable

\- \[ ] PowerPoint Board Pack is usable

\- \[ ] Customer branding is correct



\## User Acceptance



\- \[ ] General Manager/Mine Manager can use the dashboard

\- \[ ] Navigation is understandable

\- \[ ] Reports can be generated without developer assistance

\- \[ ] Customer users understand KPI status and trends

\- \[ ] Critical usability issues are resolved



\## Business Value



\- \[ ] Reporting time before Mine Manager AI measured

\- \[ ] Reporting time after Mine Manager AI measured

\- \[ ] Manual reporting effort reduction measured

\- \[ ] Decision-support usefulness rated by customer

\- \[ ] Customer confirms whether the product should continue after pilot
