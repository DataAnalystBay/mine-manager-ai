# 1. Purpose

This guide explains how executive users should use Mine Manager AI Version 1.0 to monitor mine performance, review operational risks, and support management decision-making.

Mine Manager AI is designed as an executive decision-support platform. It does not directly control mining equipment, processing plants, dispatch systems, or safety-critical operations.

---

# 2. Executive Workflow

A recommended daily workflow is:

1\. Log in.

2\. Confirm the correct company and mine.

3\. Review Mine Health.

4\. Review Production KPIs.

5\. Review Fleet KPIs.

6\. Review Plant KPIs.

7\. Review Safety KPIs.

8\. Review AI Executive Insights.

9\. Review Predictive Intelligence.

10\. Review Executive Actions.

11\. Generate or review Executive Reports.

12\. Escalate significant operational risks.

---

# 3. Dashboard

The Dashboard provides an executive overview of the operation.

Key information includes:

\- Mine Health Score

\- Production Performance

\- Fleet Performance

\- Plant Performance

\- Safety Performance

\- Executive AI Insights

\- Predictive Intelligence

\- Executive Actions

\- Operational Trends

Review the Dashboard at the beginning of each shift or management meeting.

---

# 4. Mine Health

Mine Health summarizes the overall operational condition of the mine.

A lower score should prompt review of:

\- Production

\- Fleet

\- Plant

\- Safety

\- Executive Actions

\- AI Insights

Mine Health should always be interpreted together with supporting KPI information.

---

# 5. Executive AI Insights

Executive AI Insights provide operational interpretation rather than raw data.

Typical insights include:

\- KPI movement

\- Operational risks

\- Possible root causes

\- Recommended actions

\- Executive priorities

AI insights support decision-making but do not replace engineering judgement.

---

# 6. Predictive Intelligence

Predictive Intelligence forecasts likely operational trends.

Examples include:

\- Mine Health trend

\- KPI direction

\- Forecast confidence

\- Potential operational deterioration

Forecasts should support planning discussions rather than automatic operational decisions.

---

# 7. Executive Actions

Executive Actions allow managers to:

\- Create actions

\- Assign owners

\- Track progress

\- Update status

\- Review priorities

Recommended workflow:

Open → Assigned → In Progress → Completed

Review outstanding actions daily.

---

# 8. Executive Reports

Executive Reports provide management summaries including:

\- Daily Executive Report

\- Weekly Operations Report

\- Monthly KPI Pack

\- PDF Export

\- Excel Export

Before distribution confirm:

\- Reporting period

\- Company branding

\- KPI values

\- Mine name

\- Confidentiality

---

# 9. Executive Meetings

Suggested daily meeting agenda:

1\. Mine Health

2\. Safety

3\. Production

4\. Fleet

5\. Plant

6\. Executive AI Insights

7\. Predictive Intelligence

8\. Outstanding Executive Actions

9\. New operational risks

10\. Agreed actions

---

# 10. Best Practices

\- Review Mine Health every day.

\- Investigate declining KPI trends.

\- Confirm AI insights against operational data.

\- Keep Executive Actions updated.

\- Review predictive trends regularly.

\- Validate reports before distribution.

\- Escalate critical issues promptly.

---

# 11. Limitations

Version 1.0 does not provide:

\- Autonomous mine control

\- Direct equipment control

\- SCADA control

\- Dispatch control

\- Automatic operational decisions

\- Final integrated AI Daily Briefing
